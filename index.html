Quest reminders

Tap “Set reminder” on any quest to choose a time and weekdays. While Life System is open, it checks for due reminders. Notifications require permission and a supported browser. These are not reliable background alarms: closing the app, locking the phone or suspending the browser may prevent or delay alerts. iPhone background push requires a separate hosted push service.
